About Us

About Job Circular.bd

Job Circular.bd is a job information platform focused on helping job seekers in Bangladesh discover the latest employment and career opportunities.

We publish information about government, private, banking, NGO, international, remote, part-time, fresher and internship opportunities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make job searching easier and more accessible by bringing useful employment information together in one place.

What Makes Us Different

Job Circular.bd focuses on presenting job information in a simple and easy-to-understand format so that job seekers can quickly understand the opportunity and application requirements.

Our Information Policy

We aim to provide useful and accurate job information based on available recruitment announcements and sources. Job seekers should always verify important details and application instructions from the relevant employer or official recruitment source before applying.
