About Job Circular.bd

Job Circular.bd is a job information platform focused on making career opportunities easier to discover for job seekers across Bangladesh.

We regularly publish job circulars covering government, private, banking, NGO, international, remote, part-time, fresher and other career opportunities.

  • Regular job and career updates
  • Clear qualification and eligibility information
  • Salary, location and deadline details when available
  • Application instructions and source links
  • Opportunities for freshers and experienced candidates

Our goal

To make job searching easier, faster and more accessible by bringing useful recruitment information together in one place.

We encourage every applicant to check the original employer notice before submitting an application or personal information.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Job Circular.bd may receive information voluntarily provided when visitors contact us, such as a name, email address or message. We do not ask users to submit passwords, OTPs, bank PINs or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, improve services, address technical issues and communicate about relevant requests.

3. Job Application Information

Applications are normally submitted to the employer or a third-party application platform. We are not the employer unless explicitly stated. Review the destination website's privacy policy before submitting personal information.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to Facebook, LinkedIn, Google Forms, employer websites and other third-party services. We do not control their privacy practices.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when our services or practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

1. Service

Job Circular.bd provides job and career information for general informational purposes. We are not responsible for hiring decisions made by employers.

2. Accuracy

We try to provide useful and up-to-date information, but vacancies, salaries, requirements and deadlines may change. Verify important details with the original employer or official recruitment source.

3. Third-Party Websites

Application links may direct users to external websites. We are not responsible for their content, availability, security or privacy practices.

4. User Responsibility

Users should provide accurate information when applying for jobs and should never share passwords, OTPs, bank PINs or confidential credentials with untrusted parties.

5. Fraud Awareness

Be cautious of anyone asking for money in exchange for a job. Verify recruitment information directly with the employer.

6. No Employment Guarantee

Publishing a job circular does not guarantee interview selection, employment or hiring.
